SGSR temporal shaders include code based on AMD FidelityFX Super Resolution.

The source references in Source/src/3PassCS identify FSR and FSR2 algorithms used by the conversion, activation, and upscaling passes. The sharpening pass uses the FSR RCAS algorithm.

Upstream sources:
https://github.com/GPUOpen-Effects/FidelityFX-FSR
https://github.com/GPUOpen-Effects/FidelityFX-FSR2
